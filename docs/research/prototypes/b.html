Scale: your project = 1 blast radius
Linux: bubblewrap · Landlock · seccomp
macOS: sandbox-exec

Your project is the cell. Nothing else is in it.

ai-jail starts the agent inside walls the kernel enforces. The project is writable, your toolchains come along, and your keys, shell secrets and home stay outside, on the other side of the wall.
