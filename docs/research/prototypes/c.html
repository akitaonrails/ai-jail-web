Lock the agent in. Keep the keys out.

ai-jail starts your coding agent inside a cell the operating system builds. The project is inside with it. Your SSH keys, cloud credentials and shell secrets are not.

$ ai-jail claude

InstallDo I need it?
01 · What goes in

Your project, read and write. Your toolchains and their caches. The agent's own login, so it starts signed in.

02 · What stays out

Your home directory, ~/.ssh, ~/.aws, browser profiles, every token in your shell. They are not hidden; they are not there.

03 · What it is not

A wall against a trusted agent's mistakes, so YOLO mode stays inside the project. Not a cage for hostile code. For that, a throwaway VM.
